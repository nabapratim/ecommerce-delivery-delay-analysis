# Executive Summary — E-Commerce Delivery Delay Analysis

## Objective

Evaluate the impact of delivery delays on customer satisfaction, operational performance, and revenue, and identify the primary drivers of delays to inform business decisions.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Delay Significantly Impacts Customer Experience

- Late deliveries reduce average review scores by **~1.7–2.0 points**
- High delay orders result in **~69% bad reviews**, compared to **~15% for slight delays**
- Statistical validation confirms a **highly significant relationship (p < 0.001)**

👉 **Conclusion:** Delivery delay is the **primary driver of customer dissatisfaction**

---

### 2. Logistics is the Core Bottleneck

- Logistic delay contributes **~75% of total delay**
- Seller delay contributes only **~25%**
- Paired hypothesis testing confirms a **significant difference (p < 0.001)**

👉 **Conclusion:** Operational inefficiencies are **logistics-driven, not seller-driven**

---

### 3. Revenue Exposure from Delays

- Total revenue: **~$15.5M**
- Revenue impacted by late deliveries: **~$1.16M (7.5%)**

👉 **Conclusion:** A meaningful portion of revenue is **at risk due to delays**

---

### 4. Improvement Opportunity (High → Slight Delay)

- Average rating improvement: **+1.87 points**
- Reduction in bad reviews: **~2,977 fewer cases**
- Total rating uplift: **~10,350 points**

👉 **Conclusion:** Even partial delay reduction yields **significant CX improvement**

---

### 5. Financial Impact (Conservative Estimate)

- Assuming **5% retention improvement**
- Estimated revenue recovery: **~$44K**

👉 **Note:** This is a **lower-bound estimate** (excludes repeat purchases, LTV, and referrals)

---

### 6. Freight & Pricing Insights

- Freight cost has a **weak correlation (0.17)** with delivery time
- Price and freight have **minimal impact on customer satisfaction** compared to delay

👉 **Conclusion:** Pricing strategies are **less critical than delivery performance**

---

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Prioritize Logistics Optimization

- Improve last-mile delivery efficiency
- Optimize routing and warehouse allocation
- Evaluate carrier performance

### 2. Focus on High-Delay Orders

- Target the **~5–6% high-delay segment**
- Largest ROI opportunity in reducing extreme delays

### 3. Monitor Delay KPIs

- Track:
  - Logistic vs Seller delay contribution
  - Delay buckets (on-time, slight, high)
  - Regional delay hotspots

### 4. Regional Optimization

- Focus on high-delay states and cities identified
- Improve regional logistics infrastructure and partnerships

---

## Business Impact Summary

| Metric              | Impact          |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| Customer Rating     | ↑ +1.87 points  |
| Bad Reviews         | ↓ ~54%          |
| Revenue at Risk     | ~$1.16M         |
| Recoverable Revenue | ~$44K+          |
| Root Cause          | Logistics (75%) |

---

## Final Conclusion

Delivery delay is a **critical business lever** affecting both customer experience and revenue.  
The problem is **predominantly driven by logistics inefficiencies**, and targeted improvements in this area can deliver **high-impact returns across satisfaction, retention, and revenue**.
